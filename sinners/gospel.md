# The Gospel — Slide Headlines

1. Thou shalt not compose that which thou art not prepared to lose
2. Thou shalt recognise the four sacred types, and the four sacred types shalt thou recognise. Neither more nor fewer shalt thou count, for five is right out
3. Thou shalt not leave thy data-types where all may stumble upon them
4. Thou shalt take thy registrations unto the Composer, for Program.cs is not thy dustbin
5. Thou shalt not deployeth in debug mode and call it production, for that is an abomination
6. Thou shalt alias in English and in camelCase, and thy tongue shall not slip into Danish, not even once
7. Thou shalt dot thy translations in PascalCase, and the folder shall remain empty, as is right and proper
8. Thou shalt not cast thy media into the void, unfoldered and without purpose, like some kind of animal
9. Thou shalt never assign a Template unto the Root, for a Root that Rendereth is an Abomination, and the Path to Madness.
10. Thou shalt uSync, for without uSync thy changes are as whispers in the wind, and the wind doth not git commit
